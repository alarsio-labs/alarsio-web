---
description: End a session: what is done, what is verified, what is not, and a hand-off prompt
---

Write the hand-off. Be exact about evidence.

1. **Done** — what changed, as short bullets, with file paths.
2. **Verified** — only what you saw pass this session: the command and its result.
3. **Not verified** — what you did not run, could not run, or only read. Include any
   check that preflight skipped.
4. **Open** — decisions waiting on a person, and anything uncommitted, unpushed or
   without a PR.
5. **Hand-off prompt** — a block under 40 lines that a fresh session can paste: the
   branch, the issue, the spec and ADR numbers, what is done, what is next, and the
   rules that apply. No secrets, no long logs.

Do not commit, push or change files while wrapping up.
