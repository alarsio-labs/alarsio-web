Open https://www.alarsio.com in a fresh browser window and sign in as the owner:
**[reviewer email] / [password]**. You are the owner of "Sharma Constructions (Demo)", a sample
company with 2 sites in Lucknow.

## Task 1: Approve a day's attendance (3 min)
1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Open the **10 Nov** day for **Jankipuram Shop Complex**. It is waiting for approval and was recorded by the site supervisor on the phone.
3. Check the five workers, then click **Approve**.

**Expected:** the day shows **Approved**. It can no longer be edited; any change now needs a correction with a reason.

## Task 2: Review a report that needs action (3 min)
1. Open **Daily reports**.
2. Open the **10 Nov** report for **Jankipuram Shop Complex**. It is flagged as a serious blocker: cement is down to 12 bags.
3. Click **Mark reviewed**.
4. Then open **Materials** and choose the Jankipuram site.

**Expected:** the report shows **Reviewed**. In Materials, **Cement PPC (ACC)** shows 12 bags with a **Low stock** tag (its level is 30).
