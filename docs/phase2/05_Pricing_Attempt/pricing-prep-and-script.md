# Pricing attempt: prep, call script and notes sheet

## The ask (team to confirm; Manish may change it)
**₹999 per active site per month, billed monthly.**
- Included: the web dashboard for the owner, the Android app for unlimited supervisors, attendance, reports, materials, wage sheets, and the data export.
- A site that is paused or finished is not billed.
- Example: 5 running sites = ₹4,995 per month.
- Starting offer (optional, decide before the call): first month free for design partners, then ₹999 per site.

## Why ₹999 (how we arrived at the number)
- **Alternatives today:** paper registers, Excel and WhatsApp cost ₹0 in money but a lot in time and disputes.
  - Powerplay, the closest app, is about ₹72k–1.2L a year (₹6k–10k a month), per software directories (docs/research/powerplay-2026-11.md).
- **One site's monthly labour bill:** 10 workers × 26 days × ₹600 is about ₹1.5 lakh. ₹999 is about 0.6% of that.
- **Costs to ASK the owner** (use their numbers in the doc, not ours):
  - How many wage arguments in a month, and how many days' wages they settle "to keep peace"? One disputed day for 10 workers is about ₹6,000.
  - How many cement bags or how much steel can't be accounted for? 10 bags × ₹380 is about ₹3,800.
  - How many hours a week does the owner spend driving to sites or on the phone to know what happened?
  - How many hours does the munshi or accountant spend making the wage sheet?

## Before the call
- Meet the person who decides spending: the owner, not the supervisor.
- Pick a lead (asks) and a note-taker (writes exact words). Ask: "Can we record this? It's only for our college report." Record only with a yes.
- Note the day: date, in person or phone, start and end time, who was there.

## Call script (10–15 min; Hindi is fine)
1. **How it's going:** "Since [date], your supervisor has used Alarsio at [site]. What has changed for you?" Note numbers.
2. **The cost today:** ask the 4 cost questions above and write down their answers.
3. **The ask, stated plainly. Say the number and then stop talking:**
   "To keep using it after the pilot, it is ₹999 per site per month. You have [n] sites running, so that is ₹[n×999] a month, paid monthly. Can we start that from [1 Dec]?"
4. **Listen.** Write their first reaction word for word.
5. **For each objection:** answer once, then ask "Does that solve it?" Note it in the table.
6. **Close with a next step and a date:** yes, a counter-offer, "later" (ask when and what would make it a yes), or no (ask why).
7. **Same day:** send a WhatsApp follow-up with the price, what they said, and the next step. Screenshot it.

## Final document skeleton (2–3 pages, in the guide's order)
1. Who and when
2. Context (use and value in numbers)
3. The ask (price, billing, what's included, how we got the number; use the sections above)
4. Their response (quoted)
5. Objections (table)
6. Outcome and next step with a date
7. Learnings (what we change in price, packaging, pitch or product, and why)
8. Evidence (recording or notes, plus the follow-up message screenshot)

Ask at least 2–3 owners, including visited contractors who are not yet partners. Phase 3 credits pricing talks with 3 or more businesses.
