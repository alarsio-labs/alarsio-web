# Design partner: phone call script and WhatsApp messages (14 Nov)

Fill in: [Name], [Company], [site name], [date they started].

## 1. Phone call (5–7 minutes)

1. **Hello**
   "Namaste [Name] ji, this is Manish from Alarsio. Do you have 5 minutes?"
2. **Thank them**
   "Thank you for trying Alarsio at [site name]. Your feedback helps us a lot."
3. **Ask how it is going** (write down their exact words)
   - "Is your supervisor marking attendance on the app every day?"
   - "Is anything difficult or confusing?"
   - "What one thing should we add or change?"
4. **Explain what we need from them**
   "We are students at Sitare University. Alarsio is our startup project. Our teachers want to see that real businesses use it. Can you help with two small things?"
   - "Keep using the app at your site for the next few days."
   - "Send me a short message on WhatsApp saying you use Alarsio. I will send you the text; you only check it and send it back."
5. **Tell them about the call** (very important)
   "Our teacher may call you once, just to confirm you use it. Is that okay?"
6. **Price question** (optional, only if the owner is on the call)
   "After the free pilot, the price will be ₹999 per site per month. For your [n] sites that is ₹[n × 999] a month. Would you pay this from 1 December?"
   Write down their answer word for word.
7. **Close**
   "Thank you, [Name] ji. I am sending you a WhatsApp message now."

Do not ask a relative or a friend. Do not change the start date. If they say no, thank them and stop.

## 2. WhatsApp message 1: right after the call (help with the app)

```
Namaste [Name] ji 🙏
Thank you for your time today. Here is everything for Alarsio:

1. App for your supervisor (Android): www.alarsio.com/download
2. Owner dashboard (computer or phone): www.alarsio.com
3. Every day the supervisor opens the app at the site, checks in, and marks attendance. It works without internet too.

If anything is not working, just reply here or call me on [your number].
– Manish, Alarsio
```

## 4. WhatsApp message 3: thank-you after the confirmation

```

We will keep improving Alarsio with your feedback. If your supervisor has any problem with the app, message me any time.
```

## 5. WhatsApp message 4: ask for feedback (for the feedback log)

Send to the owner, and separately to the supervisor who uses the app.

```
Namaste [Name] ji 🙏
We want to make Alarsio better for you. Please reply to these 4 short questions in your own words. Hindi or English, voice note or typing, all fine.

1. What is working well in Alarsio for you?
2. What is difficult, confusing or not working?
3. What one thing should we add or change?
4. Anything else you want to tell us?

Thank you!
– Manish, Alarsio
```

Hindi version:
```
Namaste [Name] ji 🙏
Alarsio ko aapke liye behtar banana hai. In 4 sawaalon ka jawab apne shabdon mein bhejiye. Hindi ya English, voice note ya likh kar, kuch bhi chalega.

1. Alarsio mein kya achha chal raha hai?
2. Kya mushkil hai, samajh nahi aata, ya kaam nahi kar raha?
3. Ek cheez jo hum jodein ya badlein?
4. Aur kuch batana chahenge?

Dhanyavaad!

```

### How a reply goes into the feedback log
| Log column | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Date | The date of their message |
| Partner and person | [Company], [Name] (owner / supervisor) |
| What they said | Their exact words. For a voice note, write it down word for word. |
| Type | Bug (something broken), Feature (wants something new), Usability (confusing), Data or integration, Pricing or business |
| Decision | Shipped / In progress / Planned / Won't do (give the reason) |
| Shipped on, Evidence | Only if we fixed it: the date and the PR link |
| Partner's reaction | What they said after we fixed it or replied |

One answer can make 2–3 rows: put each separate point on its own row. Screenshot the chat with the number visible and keep it as evidence.
