Phone and web screens are drawn mock-ups. The final guide will use real screenshots from the pilot organisation.

What it is

An A4 handbook, about 40 pages, one chapter per module. You email it as a PDF or print it for the site office. Every chapter opens with a dark title page and then gives numbered steps with callouts.

Best for

Owners and accountants who read at a desk. Partners who want a file to keep.

Weak spot

A supervisor on site will not open a PDF. It goes out of date with every release.

4.2 · Supervisor · Phone

Mark today's register

Open the site from Today. You must be checked in first. The register lists every worker assigned to this site.

  1. Tap Mark everyone present. Every worker on the list is set to present.
  2. Change only the exceptions: half day, leave, absent, or overtime hours. Add a note for any worker if needed.
  3. Leave the screen. The register is already saved on the phone. There is no Save button to forget.
  4. When the phone finds a signal it sends the records. Each row then shows On the server.
No signal?Keep working. Records wait on the phone and go out in order. Nothing is lost if you close the app.
Needs attentionA manager approved this day before your change arrived. Your change was not applied. Ask the manager to add a correction.

On site / Daily attendance

Record and approve a day's attendance

Works offline · 3 min read · Updated 10 Nov 2026

On the phone

  1. Check in at the site. See Check in and out.
  2. Open Register and tap Mark everyone present.
  3. Tap a worker to set half day, leave, absent or overtime hours.
  4. Done. The register saves on the phone and sends when there is a signal.

On the web

  1. Open Attendance. Days that need a look are listed first.
  2. Open a day. Check the entries, any check-ins from outside the site area, and pending corrections.
  3. Choose Approve, or Reject with a reason.
  4. An approved day is locked. Later changes come in as corrections for you to review.
RuleYou cannot approve a day you recorded yourself. Another manager or the owner must approve it.

What you see

You see approved days only. They feed the wage summary for the period. Open and rejected days are not shown to you and are never paid. See Wage summaries.

The day's lifecycle

Recorded on phone→On the server→Approved→Locked · feeds wages

A rejected day goes back to the supervisor. A change to an approved day becomes a correction, which keeps the old value, the new value and the reason.
